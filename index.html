To

Tonight's specimen queue

Every diagnosis in this hospital starts with someone who can look at a slide and say exactly what it is. Tonight, that someone is you.

Most specimens arrive stained with H&E. Hematoxylin turns nuclei purple, and eosin turns cytoplasm and matrix pink. Read those colors, the shape of the cells, and how they are arranged, and the tissue will tell you its name.

When you miss one, read the tip and go back for it. That is how every pathologist in this building got good.
